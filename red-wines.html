Red Wines

Our red wines are grown on the warm northern slopes of the valley, where long afternoons ripen the fruit fully. Each wine is fermented in small open vats and matured in French and American oak for twelve to twenty months.

From the bold Estate Shiraz to the silky Pinot Noir, there is a red here for every meal and every budget. Serve them at 16 to 18 degrees and open full-bodied wines an hour before dinner.

Every red is made only from estate-grown fruit and is suitable for vegans. Order a mixed dozen of any reds and we will include a free tasting card with food matches and cellaring advice for each wine. Older back vintages are occasionally released to members, so keep an eye on our blog for limited cellar releases.
