Welcome to Vinterra Valley Winery

Family-grown wines, crafted in the valley since 1987 and delivered to your door.

Our Story

Vinterra Valley began as a small family vineyard with just four hectares of Shiraz. Today we tend more than sixty hectares of red and white varieties, yet every bottle is still picked, pressed and blended by the same family that planted the first vines. Our cool nights and warm days give the fruit natural freshness, depth and balance.

Through this online store you can buy our complete range of red wines, white wines and hand-picked accessories such as wine coolers, corkscrews and electric openers. Create an account at checkout, choose your delivery address and we will pack your order with care within two business days.

Our cellar door is also open for tastings from Wednesday to Sunday, where you can meet the winemakers, sample new releases and enjoy a cheese platter on the lawn overlooking the vines.
