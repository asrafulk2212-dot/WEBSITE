News from the vineyard, food matching ideas and practical advice from our winemaking team.

Vintage 2026: A Season of Patience

12 October 2026

A cool spring and a long, mild summer gave our grapes extra weeks on the vine this year. Slow ripening builds flavour without pushing sugar too high, so the 2026 reds are shaping up to be elegant and perfumed. Our winemaker describes the Shiraz in barrel as the most balanced she has seen in a decade. The first wines will be released next spring.

Matching Wine with Summer Food

28 September 2026

Light whites love fresh seafood. Try our Sauvignon Blanc with prawns or a zesty salad, and the Dry Riesling with Thai-style dishes. For barbecues, a slightly chilled Pinot Noir works with everything from grilled salmon to lamb skewers, while the Estate Shiraz stands up to a smoky steak. Do not forget a cooler bag to keep whites cold outdoors.

How to Store Wine at Home

3 September 2026

Keep bottles lying on their side, away from sunlight, at a steady 12 to 16 degrees. Avoid the kitchen bench and the top of the fridge, where heat and vibration age wine quickly. Once opened, re-cork the bottle and refrigerate it; most wines stay fresh for three to five days.
