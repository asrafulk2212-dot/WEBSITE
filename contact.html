We would love to hear from you. Visit the cellar door for a tasting, call our friendly team, or send an enquiry using the form below and we will reply within one business day. Our team can help with orders, gifts, tastings, events and wholesale supply.

Visiting Us

The cellar door is a ninety-minute drive from the city. Follow the highway north, take the Vinterra Valley exit and look for our signs after the old stone bridge. Regional buses stop at the town centre, a short taxi ride away.

Bookings are not needed for standard tastings, but groups of eight or more should call ahead. Our cellar door offers free parking, wheelchair access and a shaded picnic lawn. For wholesale, restaurant and event enquiries, please mention your business name in your message and our sales manager will be in touch.

Questions about an existing order? Include your order number so we can find it quickly and give you an update on delivery. We also welcome feedback on our wines, website and service, because every comment helps us improve.
