Delivery and Returns

We deliver to every state and territory, including regional areas. You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your parcel leaves the winery. Metropolitan orders usually arrive within three to five business days, and regional orders within seven.

Orders are packed in protective, recyclable cartons and dispatched within two business days. Delivery is a flat $12.00 anywhere in Australia, and free when your order is over $150.00. Someone aged 18 or over must sign for the parcel, so please choose an address where an adult will be available.

If a bottle arrives damaged or a wine is faulty, contact us within fourteen days and we will replace it or refund you in full. Accessories are covered by a twelve-month warranty against manufacturing defects.
