White Wines

Our white wines come from the cooler, higher blocks of the vineyard. Grapes are picked in the early morning, pressed gently and fermented slowly at low temperatures to keep their bright fruit and natural acidity.

Choose a crisp Sauvignon Blanc for summer afternoons or our barrel-aged Chardonnay for richer dishes. Serve whites chilled to 8 to 12 degrees and enjoy within three years of vintage.

All of our whites are sealed with screw caps to lock in freshness, so there is no risk of cork taint and no corkscrew needed. They pair beautifully with seafood, chicken, salads and soft cheeses, and they make a thoughtful gift for summer gatherings, birthdays and long lunches with friends. Mix and match any six whites to unlock our half-case price at the cellar door, or ask our team to suggest a selection for your next dinner party.
